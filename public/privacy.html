AI Web Tool Service (“we,” “us”) uses the information described here to provide and support its AI receptionist service.

Information we handle

When you request a trial or subscribe, we receive your email, phone number if provided at checkout, and subscription status from Stripe. Call audio is handled by the phone and AI providers needed to operate the live conversation.

How information is used

We use this information to set up and provide the receptionist, send call summaries and booking follow-ups, manage billing, respond to support requests, and maintain service security. We do not sell personal information.

We rely on service providers for payment processing, phone and messaging, AI responses, hosting, and database storage. They process information as needed to provide those services. We retain account and service records while needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security.
